About Spotlight

What Spotlight is

Spotlight is a media & marketing intelligence agency. It runs digital marketing, PR & coverage monitoring, creative production and influencer coordination through one connected system — automation handles the repetitive pipes, and a human approves every decision that actually matters.

Unlike a traditional agency that staffs up for every repetitive task, Spotlight is built systems-first: open, API- and webhook-driven tooling that's ready to plug into an agentic automation stack from day one, with a strict rule that nothing publishes, spends, or sends without a person clicking approve.
